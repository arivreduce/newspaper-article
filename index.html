Breaking: Man Makes Questionable Squirrel Hats

Purchasers of the Hats Say They're a Little Too Realistic

By Play-Do Jason

In a quiet cul-de-sac just outside of Boise, a man known only as Greg has become the unlikely talk of the town—and now, the internet—after launching a line of disturbingly lifelike squirrel hats. What began as a quirky Etsy shop promising "woodland whimsy for the discerning head" has spiraled into an ethical and aesthetic debate that even Greg admits he "didn't entirely see coming." The hats, made from synthetic fur and eerily glassy eyes, are sculpted to resemble squirrels mid-scamper, some even clenching tiny faux acorns in their paws. “I just wanted to honor the humble squirrel,” Greg said, adjusting what appeared to be a juvenile gray variant perched atop his own head.

Buyers, however, are divided. While some claim the hats are “avant-garde genius,” others have expressed discomfort at their hyperrealism. "My dog hasn't stopped barking at me since I wore it home," said one customer. Another review, accompanied by a blurry photo of the hat on a park bench, simply read: “People screamed.” Animal rights activists have weighed in, praising Greg for using no real animal parts but encouraging him to maybe... dial it back. Meanwhile, Greg insists he's just getting started, teasing a new line of raccoon balaclavas “with little hands that hold your AirPods.”
